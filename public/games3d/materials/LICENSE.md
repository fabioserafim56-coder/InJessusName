# Material textures — CC0 1.0

The six WebP texture maps in this directory are delivery conversions of material textures from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/). Poly Haven's [asset license page](https://polyhaven.com/license) dedicates its assets to **CC0 1.0 Universal**, including commercial use and redistribution. The CC0 legal text is preserved without edits in [CC0-1.0.txt](CC0-1.0.txt), retrieved from [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt).

Attribution is included for provenance and respect for the creators; it is not presented as a CC0 condition or an endorsement of this app. These are contemporary material photographs/maps used for an artistic game. They do not authenticate any ancient building, location or biblical event.

Poly Haven website branding, text and logos are separate from the CC0 assets and have not been incorporated here. Bible text, other artwork and the game's code retain their own terms.

The manifest [proveniencia.json](proveniencia.json) records source URLs, official upstream MD5 checks, source/output SHA-256, dimensions, authors and the exact preparation settings. The original 1024px JPEG source files are not shipped in this directory. Albedos were encoded to 1024px WebP at quality 90; OpenGL normal maps were resized to 512 px and encoded to WebP at quality 100. Both are documented lossy encodings; quality 100 does not mean lossless. No model generated, restored or invented additional detail.
